Achievements 🏆

2nd place, Global Game Jam

With Team 5 Braincells I placed 2nd at the Global Game Jam hosted by CEGA (Center of Excellence in Gaming & Animation), representing Gaming and Animation of the CSIT department. The win qualifies us for the next round in Islamabad, where the top CEGA Karachi teams face CEGA Lahore.

Our game, Masketeer: two parallel worlds, a safe but incomplete real world and a dangerous underworld visible only when the player wears a magical mask. The underworld is a maze of traps, enemies and coins, and players must collect keys to unlock paths in the real world and finish the level.
